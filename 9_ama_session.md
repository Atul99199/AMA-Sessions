## 1. How many ways are there to connect with Claude?
Claude can be accessed through the web, desktop and mobile apps, API, and integrations with other tools. The available options depend on your needs and platform.

## 2. What are some examples of AI tools used to generate code?
Examples include GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, and Google Gemini. These tools help developers write, explain, debug, and improve code.

## 3. What is Research in Claude?
Research in Claude is a feature that performs multi-step searches across the web and available connected sources. It gathers information from multiple sources and provides a detailed answer with citations.

## 4. What are Claude Skills?
Claude Skills are reusable instructions, workflows, and resources that help Claude perform specific tasks consistently. They can be used for tasks such as coding, writing, and data analysis.

## 5. What is a project in Claude AI?
A Project in Claude is a dedicated workspace where you can organize related chats, upload files, and add custom instructions. It helps Claude maintain relevant context for a particular task or topic.

## 6. What is a hallucination in AI?
An AI hallucination occurs when an AI model generates incorrect, misleading, or fabricated information and presents it as true. Always verify important information using reliable sources.

## 7. What are the four competencies in AI fluency?
The four competencies are **Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence**. They help people assign tasks to AI, communicate requirements, evaluate AI outputs, and use AI responsibly.
